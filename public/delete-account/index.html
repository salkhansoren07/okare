Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Okare customer account and associated personal data without installing or signing in to the app.

This page is provided by Okare Enterprise, the operator of Okare.

Send your deletion request

  1. Email support@okare.in with the subject Okare account deletion request.
  2. Include your registered mobile number with country code or your registered email address. Where possible, send from the email linked to your account.
  3. Our support team will verify account ownership before processing deletion. If you no longer have access to your registered phone or email, mention that so we can help with verification.
Open email request  ↗

Opening the email does not submit a request. Add your account details and press Send in your email app. If no email app opens, use your usual email service and the address above.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, card details or identity documents in your initial email. A reason for leaving is not required.

Prefer to request in the app?

Open Profile → App settings → Delete account, verify your registered phone or password, and confirm. This schedules deletion after 30 days. An hourly server job processes eligible requests on a subsequent run.

You can cancel before processing. Signing in does not cancel the request. Active deliveries, unresolved payment/support cases, retention holds or issues requiring review can delay completion. The app shows the scheduled date and any recorded blocker.

You do not need to install the app to request deletion: the email option above is available to everyone. Email requests are handled by support; sending an email does not automatically start the in-app countdown.

What deletion covers

Your request covers your login account and associated personal data that does not need to be retained, including:

  • Profile and saved delivery addresses.
  • Favorites and account preferences.
  • Feedback and push notification registrations.
  • Personal contact, address and delivery-note details in retained order/payment records, subject to the exceptions below.

After deletion, you will no longer be able to access this account or its order history.

Records that may be retained

Limited order, payment, refund and dispute records may need to be kept for accounting, legal obligations, fraud prevention or an unresolved claim.

We will explain any applicable exception and retention period when handling your request. Financial records are not all covered by a 30-day or 90-day deletion rule. Read our privacy policy retention section for the current categories and rules.

Service-provider logs and backups follow separate retention schedules. We cannot promise immediate removal from those systems; ask support for the applicable details.

What happens next?

Support will reply with verification steps and the expected completion timeframe, then confirm when deletion is complete. This page initiates a support request; it does not automatically delete your account.

You can still request deletion if you have an active delivery, pending payment, refund or support ticket. We will explain which details need to be kept while that issue is resolved. Account deletion does not itself cancel an order or request a refund.

For updates, reply to your original email thread at support@okare.in.
